The Plotgrid View provides a grid-based overview of your story, showing which characters appear in which scenes. This helps you track character arcs, identify missing appearances, and balance screen time.

## Grid Layout

- **Rows** represent characters
- **Columns** represent scenes in reading order
- **Cells** indicate whether a character appears in a scene

## Using the Plotgrid

- **Click a cell** to toggle character presence in a scene
- **Click a scene column** to open the scene in the editor
- **Click a character row** to open the character profile
- **Drag scenes** to reorder them

## What You Can Spot

The Plotgrid makes it easy to identify:

- **Missing characters** — Characters who should appear in a scene but don't
- **Overloaded scenes** — Scenes with too many characters
- **Character gaps** — Long stretches where a character doesn't appear
- **Arc patterns** — The overall trajectory of a character's presence across the story

## Color Coding

Cells are color-coded based on the character's role in the scene:

- Primary character
- Supporting character
- Mentioned only

Configure the colors and roles in the scene metadata or inspector panel.
