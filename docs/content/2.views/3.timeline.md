The Timeline View displays your scenes and events in chronological order, helping you track the flow of time in your story and identify pacing issues.

## Timeline Modes

- **Day Mode** — Shows events on a daily timeline
- **Month Mode** — Aggregates events by month for a broader view
- **Year Mode** — High-level yearly overview

Switch between modes using the mode selector in the timeline toolbar.

## Swimlanes

Timeline Swimlanes allow you to separate events by different categories, such as:

- Plotlines
- Characters
- Locations

This helps you track parallel storylines and see how events interweave across different threads.

## Events

Events are time-stamped markers that can be attached to scenes or exist independently. They help you track:

- When scenes take place relative to each other
- Real-world dates vs. in-story dates
- Historical events in your story world

### Managing Events

- Create events directly on the timeline
- Attach events to scenes
- Set ownership to link events to specific projects
- Drag and drop to adjust timing

## Pacing Analysis

The Timeline View includes pacing analysis tools that help you:

- Identify gaps in your timeline where nothing happens
- Spot clusters of events that might be too dense
- Balance the distribution of scenes across your story's timeline

## Reading Order vs. Chronological Order

Störyline distinguishes between:

- **Reading Order** — The order scenes appear in the final manuscript
- **Chronological Order** — The actual timeline order of events

The Timeline View shows chronological order, while the Board and Manuscript views use reading order. This is useful for stories with flashbacks, non-linear narratives, or multiple timelines.
