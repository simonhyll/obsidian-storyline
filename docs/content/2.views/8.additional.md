## Plotlines View

The Plotlines View lets you manage and organize the plotlines in your story. Each plotline represents a thematic thread or storyline that runs through your scenes.

- Create and rename plotlines
- Assign colors to plotlines for visual organization
- Move scenes between plotlines
- Track setup and payoff across plotlines

## Codex Hub

The Codex Hub is a central entry point for all your reference materials. It provides quick access to:

- Characters
- Locations
- Custom codex categories

Organize your codex with custom categories and visual groups.

## Stats View

The Stats View tracks your writing progress with:

- Total word count across all scenes
- Word count per scene
- Scene completion statistics
- Writing goals and progress tracking

## Navigator View

The Navigator View provides a high-level overview of your project structure:

- Project hierarchy
- Scene organization
- Quick navigation between sections

## Story Graph

The Story Graph visualizes the connections between scenes, characters, and locations as an interactive graph. Use it to:

- See the overall structure of your story
- Identify isolated scenes or characters
- Discover unexpected connections

## Relationship Map

The Relationship Map shows character relationships as an interactive diagram. It helps you:

- Visualize character dynamics
- Identify missing relationships
- Plan character interactions
